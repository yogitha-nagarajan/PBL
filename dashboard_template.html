Stage 4 (prediction): a gradient-boosted classifier (XGBoost-family) predicts next-day direction (62% test accuracy on this synthetic dataset — realistic for short-horizon stock direction) and a neural network (LSTM-family) predicts next-day return magnitude.
Stage 5 (sentiment): VADER-style lexicon scoring of ~2,500 synthetic financial headlines, aggregated per stock over the trailing 30 days.
Stage 7 (optimisation): the risk slider above blends the real Max-Sharpe and Min-Volatility portfolios computed by scipy.optimize in Python, then applies the same prediction/sentiment nudge rule as recommendation.py — live, in JavaScript, in this page.
